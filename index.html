📋 How to Use
VIRO GGG Sticker Sheet Generator — v2.0
1

Fill in Your Details

Use the edit panel at the bottom to enter your Company Name, Short Badge (the large text on the sticker body), Role, Tagline, and Phone Number. Every sticker across all 3 pages updates instantly as you type — no save button needed.

2

Set Production Date

Pick the Production Date from the date picker. The Expiry Date auto-calculates to 30 days later and fills in automatically — the gold AUTO badge confirms it. The Expiry Date field is locked and cannot be manually edited.

3

Auto-Save — Your Data Is Always Safe

All your form fields are automatically saved in your browser as you type. Next time you open this file, everything is restored instantly. A green toast confirms restoration. No internet needed for this — it works entirely offline via your browser's storage.

4

Preview Your Sticker Sheets

Scroll through the 3 A4 pages on screen to preview all sizes:
• Page 1 — 2 Large stickers (15cm × 10cm)
• Page 2 — 8 Medium stickers (10cm × 6cm)
• Page 3 — 18 Small stickers (6cm × 4cm)
Dashed cut lines are shown between stickers on all pages.

5

Export or Print

Choose your output from the panel buttons:
• 📄 EXPORT PDF — Generates a bold, print-ready PDF with all 3 pages. Requires internet on first use (then works offline).
• 💾 SAVE FILE — Downloads the HTML file with all your current data embedded. Open it anytime — no internet needed.
• 🖨️ PRINT NOW — Opens the browser print dialog directly.

6

Offline & Online Mode

This app is offline-first. On first open (online), jsPDF is downloaded and cached in your browser. After that, all features including PDF export work without internet. A status bar at the top shows your connection state — green flashes online, red stays visible when offline. If offline and PDF hasn't been cached yet, connect once and reload.

7

Print Settings

When printing, use these settings for best results:
• Paper size: A4
• Margins: None
• Background graphics: Enabled (so colours & gradients print)
• Scale: 100% (do not scale to fit)
Use a colour printer for full quality.

💡 Tips & Notes
  • The PDF uses large, bold typography — company name, badge, phone, and dates are all clearly readable at actual sticker size.
  • For best print quality, use 160gsm sticker paper or glossy photo paper. Matte sticker paper also works well.
  • Cut along the dashed lines after printing for clean sticker edges.
  • Use Save File to keep a backup with your data pre-filled — share it with colleagues or reuse next batch.
  • The PDF export includes all 3 pages in one file — ideal for sending directly to a print shop.
  • Your data is auto-saved in this browser and restored on next open.
  • Fonts fall back to system fonts (Impact / Arial) if Google Fonts is unavailable offline — layout stays intact.
